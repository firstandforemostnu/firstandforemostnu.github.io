Writing from the first in our families to get here.

The name of the magazine First and Foremost was born out of our motivations to make the publication. At a predominantly white, wealthy private university, it is easy for the struggles of marginalized students to go unaddressed. In particular, first-gen, low-income, and/or undocumented students have rarely had a place to voice their opinions and feelings.

First, for the students in this publication will be accomplishing many firsts in college, the first in their family to be going to university, the first to speak up against inequality, and the first to prioritize themselves.

Foremost, for the importance that these students have at our university and for the place that they deserve at the table that they have not had before. First and Foremost was created to be a platform for these students, to share their creativity, passions, and thoughts.
